## Risk Warnings and Precautions

Cryptocurrency investment carries high risks, and investors need to fully understand related risks before participating:

### Main Risks

1. **Price Volatility Risk**: Cryptocurrency prices may fluctuate dramatically in short periods
2. **Technology Risk**: Technical issues like smart contract vulnerabilities and network attacks may cause losses
3. **Regulatory Risk**: Regulatory policies for cryptocurrencies in various countries may change
4. **Liquidity Risk**: Some small-cap coins may have insufficient liquidity issues
5. **Market Manipulation Risk**: Markets may have manipulation behaviors affecting price movements

### Investment Recommendations

To succeed in the cryptocurrency market, we recommend that investors:

1. **Fully Understand the Market**: Deeply understand related knowledge before investing
2. **Control Investment Proportion**: Only invest spare money that doesn't affect normal life
3. **Diversify Investment**: Don't put all funds into a single asset
4. **Set Stop-losses**: Strictly implement risk management measures
5. **Continuous Learning**: Keep up with market developments and continuously improve investment skills
6. **Stay Calm**: Remain calm during severe market volatility and avoid emotional decisions
