## Trade Background

**Period**: Nov 1-30, 2024  
**Ticker**: AAPL (Apple Inc.)  
**Strategy**: The Wheel (CSP + Covered Call)  
**Initial Capital**: $2,000  
**Target**: 10%+ monthly return

---

## Day 1 (Nov 1): Open CSP

### Market Conditions
- AAPL price: $175
- Market sentiment: Choppy, slightly bullish
- IV Percentile: 45% (moderate volatility)

### Trade Decision
**Sell 1 Dec 1st $170 Put**
- Expiration: 30 days (Dec 1)
- Strike: $170 (support level)
- Delta: 0.28
- Premium collected: $2.80/share × 100 = **$280**

### Rationale
1. $170 is key support (200-day MA)
2. Delta 0.28 implies 72% win rate
3. Even if assigned, cost basis only $167.20 ($170-$2.80)

---

## Day 7 (Nov 8): Stock Pullback

### Market Change
- AAPL drops to $172 (-1.7%)
- Put value: $3.50 (floating loss $70)

### Action
**Hold**

### Reason
- Price still above $170 support
- Theta decaying $8-10 daily
- Time is on my side

---

## Day 14 (Nov 15): Theta Acceleration

### Market Status
- AAPL rebounds to $176
- Put value: $1.50 (floating profit $130)

### Decision Point
**Consider early close?**

**Analysis:**
- Remaining premium: $1.50
- Days left: 16
- Expected gain if hold: $1.50
- Profit if close now: $130

**Decision: Continue holding**
- Let final 2 weeks of Theta work
- Save transaction costs

---

## Day 21 (Nov 22): Week Before Expiry

### Market Status
- AAPL stable at $177
- Put value: $0.50 (floating profit $230)

### Action
**Buy back Put to close**
- Pay $0.50 × 100 = $50
- Net profit: $280 - $50 = **$230**
- Free up capital early

### Rationale
1. Only $50 time value left, not worth waiting
2. Free up capital 7 days early for next round
3. Avoid expiration uncertainty

---

## Key Decision Points Review

### Decision 1: Why $170 strike?
✅ **Right choice**
- Strong technical support
- Sufficient safety margin
- Delta 0.28 high win rate

### Decision 2: Why hold when down $70 on Day 7?
✅ **Right patience**
- Price didn't break support
- Theta working continuously
- Floating loss eventually became profit

### Decision 3: Why close early on Day 21?
✅ **Smart move**
- Too little time value remaining
- Early capital release more efficient
- Avoid expiration risks

### Decision 4: Why open new position before first expired?
✅ **Capital efficiency optimization**
- 7 days earlier = 7 more days of Theta
- Compound effect maximized

---

## Lessons Learned

### ✅ What Went Right

1. **Right stock**: AAPL good liquidity, moderate volatility
2. **Reasonable strike selection**: Sufficient safety margin
3. **Patient holding**: Not scared by short-term moves
4. **Active management**: Early close to free capital

### ❌ Room for Improvement

1. **Could be more aggressive**: Delta 0.28 conservative, could try 0.3-0.35
2. **No Collar hedge**: Vulnerable to black swans
3. **Single ticker risk**: Should diversify 2-3 tickers
