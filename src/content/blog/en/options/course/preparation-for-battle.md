## I. Capital Anxiety: Am I Too Poor to Trade Options?

This is the most common DM I receive: _"SilentXx, I only have $2000 USD, am I not qualified to do options?"_

The outside misconception is: options are a rich man's game.
**The truth is: options are the fastest tool for the "poor" to turn things around (or crash).**

- **If you only have $500:** Even if you can't afford 100 shares of stock, you can still be a seller through **Vertical Spreads**. Your maximum risk is locked at tens of dollars, yet return rates can reach 20%-50%.
- **If you have $5,000:** You can start trying **The Wheel Strategy**, practicing on low-priced blue chips like Ford (F) or American Airlines (AAL).
- **If you have $50,000+:** Congratulations, you can unlock all advanced plays and build a "rent collection portfolio" spanning tech, consumer, and healthcare.

**My recommendation:**
For beginners, **$2,000 - $5,000** is the most comfortable range.
Don't trade with living expenses. Options are meant to improve your life, not pay your rent.

## II. Account Permissions: Don't Get Stuck by Broker "Levels"

When you open options at Charles Schwab, IBKR, or Futu, they'll ask you to choose a Level.
Choose wrong, and you might not even be able to click the order button.

- **Level 1 (Covered Call):** Most basic level. Only allows you to sell Calls when you own stock. **This is kindergarten for beginners.**
- **Level 2 (Cash Secured Put / Long Options):** Allows you to buy options and sell Puts (as long as you have sufficient cash). **This is the main battleground for our beginner courses.**
- **Level 3 (Spreads):** Allows spreads. This is where capital efficiency takes a leap. **Essential for intermediate courses.**
- **Level 4 (Naked Selling):** Naked selling. Allows you to "hustle with nothing" without money or stock. **This is the shortcut to the rooftop; beginners absolutely shouldn't open this!**

**SilentXx's Recommendation:**
Start by applying for **Level 2**. After you're proficient, apply for Level 3. As for Level 4, wait until you've traded for 3 years.

## III. Tool Selection: A Good Craftsman Needs Good Tools

Don't do options with those crude stock-only apps. You need professional software that can display the **"Option Chain"** and **"Greeks"**.

1.  **Futu/Tiger (Moomoo/Tiger):** Most Chinese-friendly interface, unbeatable mobile experience. Perfect for beginners.
2.  **Interactive Brokers (IBKR):** Globally strongest, lowest fees, highest interest, but interface looks like 1990s software. Suitable for hardcore players.
3.  **ThinkOrSwim (TOS):** The software American investors use. Chart analysis ceiling, but higher account opening threshold (usually requires US identity).

**No matter which you use, make sure you can see at a glance: Implied Volatility (IV) and Delta.** These two indicators are our compass to profits.
